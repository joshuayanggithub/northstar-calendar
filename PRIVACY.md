# North Star — Privacy Policy

North Star (project name `calendar-app`) is a personal desktop calendar app for macOS. It connects to your Google Calendar through Google's official Calendar API, only after you grant consent on Google's sign-in screen.

## What the app accesses
- The calendars and events of the Google account you connect, to show them and to sync the changes you make in the app (creating, editing, moving and deleting events, answering invitations).
- Nothing else from your Google account.

## Where your data is stored
- Event data is kept in a local database on your own computer, in the app's data folder.
- Your Google sign-in token is stored locally on your computer only.
- The app talks to no server other than Google's APIs. There is no analytics, advertising, telemetry or third-party service of any kind.

## Sharing
- No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
- Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data
- Disconnect the account in the app (Settings › Calendar accounts), or revoke the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Deleting the app's data folder removes the local copy of your calendar data.
